# LLD Calendar — 12 Oct to 1 Dec 2026

22 problems · 55 remaining reps · all work lands by **19 Nov** · **12 days buffer** after.

**The buffer is load-bearing.** Festivals, family, travel will cost days. The schedule
absorbs ~12 lost days without slipping. Do not spend the buffer early.

---

## How a day works

| Slot | When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | morning (strongest window) | the new problem — R1 | 2-3 h |
| **B** | afternoon | the rep(s) — R2 or R3 | 45 min each |

On a day with no R1, slot A is free — use it for DSA or recover a missed rep.

**Rule:** R2 comes ~6 days after R1. R3 comes ~16 days after R2. Already computed below —
just follow the dates.

## The three phases

**Phase 1 — 12-19 Oct (office, slow).** 2 new problems + 1 rep. Low target on purpose:
the goal is starting, not volume.

**Phase 2 — 20 Oct to 7 Nov (home, all in).** One new problem EVERY day plus reps.
This is the engine. 19 R1s in 19 days.

**Phase 3 — 8-19 Nov.** No new problems. Reps only, clearing the R2/R3 queue.

**Buffer — 20 Nov to 1 Dec.** Lost days, mocks 2 and 3, weak-problem redos, optional
problems if genuinely ahead.

## Mocks

- **Mock 1 — book now for ~27 Oct.** After 11 problems. **Unfamiliar prompt**, not from
  the list. Ask for feedback on five axes: requirements gathering, code completeness,
  OO design, time management, communication. This is the one that can still change
  11 problems of remaining practice.
- **Mock 2 — ~21 Nov.** Machine coding. Blank editor, strict timer, runnable code, tests,
  unseen extension, no hints.
- **Mock 3 — ~25 Nov.** Discussion / hybrid. Unfamiliar design; interviewer challenges
  class boundaries and forces a partial redesign.

Ask for specific observations, never a pass/fail verdict.

## Slippage rules

**Never skip a rep to stay on schedule for a new problem.** A missed R2 means the R1 was
wasted. If a day gets cut, drop the R1 and keep the rep.

| Behind by | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1-3 days | nothing, the buffer covers it |
| 4-7 days | drop problems 21-22 (Text Editor, File System — lowest frequency) |
| 8-14 days | drop 19-22, and R3 only on core problems that scored below 2 stars |
| more | keep core 12 at 3 reps, cut everything else |

**What never gets cut:** the core 12 at three reps, and all three mocks.
